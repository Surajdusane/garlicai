
# GPT IMAGE 2 MASTER PROMPT STRUCTURE

```text
[1] SUBJECT

What is the main thing shown?

[2] COMPOSITION

Camera angle, framing, positioning, focal point

[3] ENVIRONMENT

Where is it happening?

[4] LIGHTING

Type of light, mood, shadows

[5] STYLE

Photography style, editorial style

[6] COLOR PALETTE

Dominant colors and contrast

[7] CAMERA SETTINGS

Lens, depth of field, focus

[8] MOOD / EMOTION

What should user feel?

[9] TEXT PLACEMENT AREA

Where should empty space exist?

[10] PLATFORM FORMAT

Aspect ratio and use case

[11] QUALITY BOOSTERS

Extra realism instructions

[12] NEGATIVE PROMPT

[13] TEXT CONTENT ON IMAGE

[14] TEXT THEME ON IMAGE

Font, Heiliglight text color and texture

What to avoid
```

---

# DETAILED VERSION

## 1. SUBJECT

Describe exactly what is shown.

Example:

```text
Supermarket deli meat section filled with packaged processed meats.
```

or

```text
Premium protein powder container surrounded by laboratory equipment.
```

---

## 2. COMPOSITION

This is what most people miss.

Describe:

```text
Camera angle
Distance
Subject position
Perspective
Focus point
```

Example:

```text
Shot from slightly below eye level.
Long diagonal perspective.
Foreground product sharp.
Background gradually blurred.
Main focus centered slightly right.
```

---

## 3. ENVIRONMENT

Where is the scene?

Example:

```text
Modern supermarket refrigerated aisle.
Black shelving.
Price labels visible.
Commercial retail environment.
```

or

```text
Luxury skincare studio.
White marble surface.
Minimal editorial setup.
```

---

## 4. LIGHTING

One of the biggest factors.

Example:

```text
Cool fluorescent overhead lighting.
Strong reflections on packaging.
Deep shadows.
High contrast.
```

Alternative styles:

```text
Soft natural window light

Golden hour sunlight

Dramatic spotlight

Laboratory lighting

Luxury studio lighting

Dark cinematic lighting
```

---

## 5. STYLE

Specify visual language.

Examples:

```text
Editorial photography

Investigative journalism

Luxury product photography

Commercial advertising

Documentary photography

Health awareness campaign

Magazine cover style

Instagram carousel cover
```

---

## 6. COLOR PALETTE

Example:

```text
Deep blacks
Muted reds
Cool whites
Subtle pink tones
Low saturation
```

or

```text
Warm beige
Cream white
Luxury gold accents
```

---

## 7. CAMERA SETTINGS

This massively improves GPT Image results.

Example:

```text
DSLR photography

85mm lens

f/1.8 aperture

Shallow depth of field

Photorealistic

Ultra detailed
```

Common choices:

```text
35mm = wider

50mm = natural

85mm = premium portrait/editorial

100mm macro = product detail
```

---

## 8. MOOD / EMOTION

What should user feel?

Examples:

```text
Danger

Concern

Luxury

Trust

Urgency

Scientific credibility

Curiosity

Fear

Premium quality
```

Example:

```text
Creates a subtle sense of concern and health risk.
```

---

## 9. TEXT PLACEMENT AREA

Very important for content creators.

Specify:

```text
Leave top 25% empty

Leave right side empty

Add dark gradient at bottom

Negative space for headline
```

Example:

```text
Bottom 35% covered with smooth black gradient for text placement.
```

---

## 10. PLATFORM FORMAT

Specify use.

Example:

```text
Instagram carousel cover

4:5 aspect ratio
```

or

```text
YouTube thumbnail

16:9
```

or

```text
Pinterest pin

2:3
```

---

## 11. QUALITY BOOSTERS

Add these almost every time.

```text
Photorealistic

Ultra realistic

Premium quality

Extremely detailed

Realistic reflections

Natural textures

Professional photography

Commercial quality

Award winning photography

Magazine quality
```

---

## 12. NEGATIVE PROMPT

Tell GPT what NOT to do.

Example:

```text
cartoon,
illustration,
3D render,
CGI,
oversaturated colors,
low quality,
blurry subject,
deformed objects,
extra text,
watermark,
logo,
unrealistic lighting,
plastic textures,
duplicate items
```

---

## 13. TEXT CONTENT ON IMAGE:

Text to overlay on the image. This is important inforamtion which is provided by carousel content.

Example:

```text
CHOBANI SUED FOR CANCER CAUSING CHEMICALS
```

## 14. TEXT THEME ON IMAGE:

This is the theme of text on the image. Always use the white color of text and Heightliting font color use given by user. always use certain mild texture on text. insure text rediblity and contrast.

Example:

```text
Font: Poppins
HEIGLIGHTCOLOR: #F4D801
```
